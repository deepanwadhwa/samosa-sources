# S19t: durable automatic item backoff

Automatic indexing now reserves a bounded per-item cooldown before work and
finalizes failed attempts with a full delay from completion. Levels double from
one second to a60-second cap. Killed attempts retain the reservation. Automatic
selection skips cooling items, allowing ready work in the same/other jobs to
proceed; explicit reindex steps can retry immediately.

Repeated observations of unchanged content/identity preserve cooldown. Changed
verified targets clear it; matching indexed content and successful commits delete
cooldown with intent. One row per pending item bounds state to2048 rows. Public
pending replies expose the absolute next-attempt time and admitted backoff level.
Integer/range validation refuses corrupt metadata. Checked wall-clock arithmetic
preserves restart state, with far-future schedules treated due after clock rollback.

Native tests exercise actual one-second expiry, exact doubling and capped levels,
unchanged checkpoint retention, manual override, healthy peer progress,520 cooled
polls without receipt growth, changed-target recovery at a full receipt quota,
invalid-level refusal and retained history. Long-delay cases expire owned fixture
rows instead of sleeping for a minute. An actual worker is held beyond its initial
delay and then released, proving a full delay after failure; a second admitted
worker is SIGKILLed, proving its reservation remains and restart polling is idle.
Fault pauses are confined to test builds.

Broader clock/resource qualification, precheck/store-open races and complete
missing/new/failed-file/legacy policies remain open. Full Sources and C01–C14
rebuild qualification remain incomplete.
