# S19q: durable reindex attempt ordering

Reindex admission records one durable attempt per selected item before content
work. Pending selection orders by attempts, then ordinal. A missing/failed first
item retains intent but no longer monopolizes every subsequent fresh operation:
less-attempted peers proceed. Repeated watch observations preserve attempts while
work remains pending; original-byte restoration and successful version commit
clear the attempt row together with the intent. Killed attempts remain recorded.

Counters are bounded to one row per pending item, saturate at INT64_MAX and are
validated as nonnegative SQLite integers before publication or selection. Public
index_pending reports attempts. Native regression combines a missing leading file,
a changed healthy peer, repeated observations, offline status, retry counting and
restoration/reset. Private negative-counter corruption refuses a single structured
error before publication or selection.

This qualifies ordering within a selected job. Cross-job scheduling, timed backoff,
automatic execution and complete missing/new/failed-file policies remain required,
along with full Sources and C01–C14 rebuild qualification.
